Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide physiotherapy sessions at home?
Yes, we offer professional home physiotherapy services across Bangalore, allowing you to receive expert care in the comfort of your home.
What conditions do you treat?
We treat a wide range of conditions including neck pain, back pain, sciatica, frozen shoulder, knee pain, arthritis, sports injuries, post-surgical recovery, stroke rehabilitation, Parkinson's disease, and mobility disorders.
Do you offer online consultation and rehabilitation programs?
Yes, we provide online consultations and guided rehabilitation programs for patients who prefer remote care or are unable to attend in-person sessions.
Is physiotherapy helpful after surgery?
Absolutely. Our post-surgical rehabilitation programs are designed to reduce pain, restore mobility, improve strength, and support a safe recovery after surgery.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by contacting us directly through our website, phone, or WhatsApp, and we'll help schedule a session at your convenience.
